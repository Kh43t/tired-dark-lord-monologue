A 100-word villain monologue for make.fun's "The Tired Dark Lord" bounty: Malachar the dark lord receives the latest hero in his hall — and is too tired to gloat properly. The tiredness lives in the pauses, the broken chair, the two hundred stairs, the tea going cold, and the sentence he cannot quite finish. He never says he is tired. He is still a villain: the wells of Aldermere turn black at the thaw, the ravens already carry his letters, and the rebellion ends when the snow does.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue (exactly 100 words, 556 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`, after a self-check that it stays near 100 words and inside the gate's 1–600 characters.
